# Preview sources and scope

The repository contains two existing presentation exports from the designer's Cravo portfolio workspace, selected and visually inspected on 6 November 2026.

| File | Content |
| --- | --- |
| assets/cravo-cover.png | Address-entry excerpts arranged across desktop, tablet and mobile. |
| assets/cravo-responsive-showcase.png | Opening-screen compositions labelled 1600 × 900, 834 × 1194 and 390 × 844, presented at reduced scale. |

Both previews omit food and restaurant photography. They are presentation panels, not full-resolution source artboards or executable interfaces. The supplied Behance case-study link provides the broader portfolio presentation.

Addresses, restaurant ratings, review counts, delivery estimates and fees are concept data. They do not establish actual service availability, customer feedback or delivery performance.

Visual inspection covered panel boundaries, heading and caption readability, and the absence of food photography in these two exports. This inspection does not replace an accessibility or responsive audit of the underlying interface. Some embedded interface text is small at presentation scale; consult the Behance case study for the broader story.

The Figma source file and unverified photo-bearing exports are not distributed in this repository. No open-source license or third-party image redistribution permission is granted.
